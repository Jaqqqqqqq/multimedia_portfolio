Step-by-Step Tutorial

Step 1: Find a Video Reference

Select a reference video for rotoscoping. Watch the video below for guidance:

Step 2: Set Up Your Workspace

Prepare your animation software and import the reference footage. Organize your layers and establish your frame rate.

Step 3: Begin Rotoscoping

Start tracing the subject frame by frame. Use the pen tool to create precise outlines for each frame.

Step 4: Refine Details

Go back and refine the details. Ensure smooth transitions between frames and fix any inconsistencies.

Step 5: Add Effects & Polish

Apply colors, effects, and final touches to complete your rotoscope animation.
